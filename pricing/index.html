Before you choose. Straight answers.

What is a credit?

Credits measure the AI work Ocur does for you — reading, writing, working in your tools, making images. Bigger models, long outputs and media use more; cached input uses fewer. Your allowance is shared across Ocur, oDrops and oWorlds, renews every month, and the app shows how much you've used.

What happens if I run out?

Ocur stops and tells you — there are no surprise overage charges. Upgrade, add a top-up on a company plan, or wait for your allowance to renew next month.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Switch plans or cancel any time from your billing settings — no call, no form.

Can my company try Ocur before paying?

Yes. Start on a free account and hand Ocur real work. When your team joins, pick a company plan — every human member is free. Prefer a guided pilot? Talk to us.

Do we pay per person?

No. Every company plan includes unlimited human members — inviting a colleague costs nothing. You pay for a shared monthly credit pool and the AI workers, with extra AI workers at $199 a month each.
