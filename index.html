Questions, answered.

Is it really free to start?

Yes. The free plan is the full operating system with a monthly allowance of work — enough to pilot Ocur on real tasks. No credit card, no trial clock. When it proves itself, upgrading takes one click.

How is this different from a chat assistant?

A chat assistant gives you an answer — the work is still yours. Ocur finishes the job where it lives: with its own inbox and phone line, a heartbeat that keeps running while you're away, and live ledgers for your promises, money, pipeline and stock. The result lands in your tools — not your clipboard.

Do I need to install anything?

No — Ocur runs in your browser, and you can message Ocur from WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack or Discord too. Want it working on your own computer as well? A small companion app connects your machine in about a minute.

Will it do things without asking?

Only where you've allowed it. Out of the box, anything important waits for sign-off. Put a job on autopilot, though, and Ocur wakes on a heartbeat and runs the whole loop by itself — every step on the record. You can widen or narrow what runs alone at any time, quiet hours included.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

You'll see it — every action is on the record, with what was done and why. Anything important waited for your sign-off in the first place. Correct it in plain language, and Ocur remembers the correction — the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

What does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion, GitHub, WhatsApp, Telegram, Slack and Discord — plus the web itself, and even your own computer if you connect it. You add accounts one by one, and you can disconnect any of them at any time.

Is our data private?

Your company’s workspace is sealed off from every other customer’s. Ocur can only reach the accounts you’ve chosen to connect, and you can take any of them away in one click. We never sell your data and never train models on it — the privacy policy spells out exactly what happens to everything Ocur touches, Google Workspace included.

Can the whole company use it?

Yes — that's the point. Everyone delegates to one shared Ocur with shared memory and knowledge, admins decide who may do what, and there's one place to see everything that got done.

Do we pay per person?

No. Human members are always free, however many of you there are — inviting a colleague costs nothing. What you pay for is Ocur's work: a monthly allowance of it, and the AI workers that run jobs on their own schedule. Need another pair of hands? An extra AI worker is $199 a month, and it brings 25M tokens of work with it.

How long does rollout take?

Minutes, not months. Ocur runs in the browser, connects to your tools one sign-in at a time, and needs no IT project. Most teams hand it real work on day one.

Can I get a demo first?

Yes. Book a 30-minute call and the founder walks you through Ocur live, on your company's own work — bring your questions. Or skip the call: the free plan is the full system, so you can pilot it on real tasks today and talk once you've seen it.
