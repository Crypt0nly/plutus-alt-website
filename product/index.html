04A small first step

Your first ten minutes.

Ocur isn't a chat box. It's a desk — apps, a dock, and Ocur in the middle. Here's how the first ten minutes go.

  1. 1

    Sign in. You're at a desk.

    Chat, Connectors, Settings and the App Store are already there. Every other app appears the moment Ocur has something to put in it.

  2. 2

    Say the first job.

    “Find the three invoices we're still owed and draft the reminders.” Ocur works in a window beside the chat and shows you what it's doing.

  3. 3

    Connect one tool.

    Gmail, WhatsApp, Notion — one sign-in each. Ocur reads only what you connect, and you can take it away again in a click.

  4. 4

    Put it on autopilot.

    Turn the job into an automation with a schedule. Ocur runs it while you're away and reports back in your chat — or on WhatsApp.

Every window has its own link — share a thread, a list or a deal with a colleague and they land right on it.
