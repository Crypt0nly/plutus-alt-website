Getting started

Is there a free plan?

Yes. On your own, the free plan is open to everyone: Ocur Desktop and oDrops with 1.5M credits every month and access to create 2 Drops with the models you choose. No credit card, no trial clock. See every plan.

Do I need to install anything?

No — Ocur runs in your browser. Prefer a dedicated app? Download Ocur Desktop for Windows, macOS or Linux and sign in to the same workspace. An internet connection is required. The desktop app includes Ocur Companion and sets up this computer after sign-in. Screen and computer-control permissions remain yours to choose.

How long does rollout take?

Minutes, not months. Ocur runs in the browser, connects to your tools one sign-in at a time, and needs no IT project. Most teams hand it real work on day one.

Can I get a demo first?

Yes. Book a 30-minute call and the founder walks you through Ocur live, on your company's own work — bring your questions. Or start free and try it yourself first.

How Ocur works

How is this different from a chat assistant?

A chat assistant gives you an answer — the work is still yours. Ocur finishes the job where it lives: with its own inbox and phone line, a heartbeat that keeps running while you're away, and live ledgers for your promises, money, pipeline and stock. The result lands in your tools — not your clipboard.

Will it do things without asking?

Only where you've allowed it. Out of the box, anything important waits for sign-off. Put a job on autopilot, though, and Ocur wakes on a heartbeat and runs the whole loop by itself — every step on the record. You can widen or narrow what runs alone at any time, quiet hours included.

What happens when it gets something wrong?

You'll see it — every action is on the record, with what was done and why. Anything important waited for your sign-off in the first place. Correct it in plain language, and Ocur remembers the correction — the same mistake doesn't happen twice.

What does it work with?

Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Notion and GitHub — plus the web itself, and even your own computer if you connect it. Paid plans include Ocur on WhatsApp, iMessage, Telegram, Slack, Teams and email. You add accounts one by one, and you can disconnect any of them at any time.

Your company and your data

Is our data private?

Your company’s workspace is sealed off from every other customer’s. Ocur can only reach the accounts you’ve chosen to connect, and you can take any of them away in one click. We never sell your data and never train models on it — the privacy policy spells out exactly what happens to everything Ocur touches, Google Workspace included. For the plain map of which service receives what, and when, see where your data goes.

Can the whole company use it?

Yes — that's the point. Everyone delegates to one shared Ocur with shared memory and knowledge, admins decide who may do what, and there's one place to see everything that got done.

Do we pay per person?

No. Every company plan includes unlimited human members — inviting a colleague costs nothing. Your company pays for a shared monthly credit pool and AI workers with real jobs and schedules. Extra AI workers are $199 a month each. Credit top-ups add 10M credits for $100 and expire at your next monthly reset.
